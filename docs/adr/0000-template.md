# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as the chosen option

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-kebab-title.md) | Deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Source: the spec, plan or doc where the decision was made (with section)

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, the roadmap pressure.
State facts, not the answer.

## Decision

The choice, in one or two sentences, then the specifics that make it concrete
(names, boundaries, rules that code must follow).

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Option A    | Reason, as recorded in the source doc |
| Option B    | Reason                                |

If the source gives no alternatives, say so explicitly rather than inventing them.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer.

### Negative

- What becomes harder, what it costs, what risk is accepted.
